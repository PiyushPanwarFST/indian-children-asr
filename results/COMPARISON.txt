# Key: Combining children's speech knowledge (Kid-Whisper) with Indian language

# Technique Reference
  Fusion approach based on: Gated Multimodal Units (Arevalo et al., ICLR 2017)
  Dual-encoder concept inspired by: CARE (Dutta & Ganapathy, IEEE TASLP 2025)
  Knowledge distillation: Hinton et al., 2015
