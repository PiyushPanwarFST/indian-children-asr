# Key Results
  Best overall WER:      14.30% (Whisper fine-tuned) / 14.57% (our dual-encoder fusion)
  Best Hindi WER:        12.67% (our dual-encoder fusion) — 2.4% better than fine-tuning
  Acoustic → Combined:   19.96% → 14.57% (-5.39% absolute, -27% relative improvement)
  Fusion validation:     combining KD-specialized encoders outperforms either encoder alone

# Reference Papers
  Fusion approach:       Gated Multimodal Units (Arevalo et al., ICLR 2017)
  Dual-encoder concept:  CARE (Dutta & Ganapathy, IEEE TASLP 2025)
  Knowledge distillation: Hinton et al., 2015
  MuRIL teacher model:   Khanuja et al., 2021 (google/muril-base-cased)
